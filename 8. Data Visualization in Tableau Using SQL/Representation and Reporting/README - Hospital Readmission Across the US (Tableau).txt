# Hospital Readmission Across the United States

## Introduction
This dashboard enables executive leaders to identify which states have the highest number of readmissions relative to the number of hospitals, allowing them to focus on specific geographic areas and implement targeted reduction strategies. 
By identifying states with high volumes of patient readmissions, administrators can allocate funding and resources more effectively.
These insights can facilitate collaboration among hospitals, enabling them to compare and implement successful reduction strategies.

## Data Sources
1. The medical data provided contains historical data on patient readmissions across various hospitals.
2. The US Healthcare Readmissions and Mortality data sourced from Kaggle was provided by Health World and details the number of hospitals and mortality rates across the United States.

These datasets help us understand the correlation between hospital readmission rates and the number of hospitals in each state, providing a comprehensive view of healthcare performance.

## Results
The analysis revealed several key findings:
• Texas had the highest number of readmission cases, with 223 patients readmitted, and the most hospitals, totaling 360.
• California followed closely, with 210 readmitted patients and 327 hospitals.
• There is a noticeable correlation between states with more hospitals and higher readmission rates, suggesting that states with larger healthcare infrastructures may face greater challenges in managing patient readmissions.
