# Hospital Readmissions Across the United States

## Introduction
I developed a Tableau dashboard to examine readmissions and hospital counts by state using two datasets. This dashboard allows executive leaders to pinpoint states with the highest readmission rates relative to the number of hospitals, enabling them to concentrate on specific regions and implement targeted strategies to reduce readmissions. 
By identifying states with significant patient readmission volumes, administrators can more effectively allocate resources and funding.

## Results
The analysis revealed several key findings:
• Texas had the highest number of readmission cases, with 215 patients readmitted, and the most hospitals, totaling 364.
• California followed closely, with 196 readmitted patients and 331 hospitals.
• There is a noticeable correlation between states with more hospitals and higher readmission rates, suggesting that states with larger healthcare infrastructures may face greater challenges in managing patient readmissions.

In conclusion, the analysis has highlighted key areas for improvement in managing hospital readmissions, particularly in states like Texas and California. 
By focusing on these insights, hospital administrators and institutions can allocate resources more effectively and implement targeted strategies to reduce readmission.
